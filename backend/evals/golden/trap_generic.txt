ANU B.Tech. R20 Regulations
II Year I Semester
THERMAL ENGINEERING

UNIT – I (10 Hours)
Air Standard Cycles: Otto cycle; Diesel cycle; Dual combustion cycle: Air-standard efficiency, Mean effective pressure.
Actual Cycles – Deviations from air-standard cycles, Time loss factor, Heat loss factor, Exhaust blowdown.

UNIT – II (8 Hours)
Fuel Systems: Carburetion, Fuel injection systems – MPFI, TBI, GDI: Injection timing diagrams.
Ignition Systems – Battery ignition, Magneto ignition, Electronic ignition.

Text Books:
